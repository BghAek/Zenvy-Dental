## What
Implement task S1-7: Landing page hero with 3D elements, value propositions, and the pricing page.

## Why & decisions
- The 3D hero uses React Three Fiber and is lazy-loaded (`ssr: false`) through a wrapper to not block SSR or TTI, which is important for the `Lighthouse >= 90` requirement.
- The 3D element is an abstract, slowly rotating floating sphere that feels premium and matches the minimal design guidelines.
- Value props describe the core offering (WhatsApp 24/7, Automations, Easy setup) directly on the homepage.
- The Tarifs page correctly anchors the €20/month Standard plan as "Indisponible" while highlighting the €399/month Premium plan as the single purchasable plan with the 14-day free trial CTA.
- The sprint plan status is updated to 🟨 in this PR.
