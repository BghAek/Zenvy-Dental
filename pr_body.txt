## What
Implements the Owner Portal (S4-2) allowing the founder to operate v1 clinics.

## Why & decisions
This allows the administration of ZenvyDental using a custom-built interface that talks to the `SUPER_ADMIN` ops API routes.
Decisions:
- Scaffolded `apps/owner` mirroring the Vite + Tailwind setup of `apps/web`.
- Used `@zenvy/ui` for all tables, states, and inputs to keep the design premium and consistent.
- Four main routes mapped directly to the ops endpoints.

## How to test
1. Start the owner portal dev server: `pnpm --filter @zenvy/owner dev`
2. Access the portal and verify the 4 routes: `/clients`, `/errors`, `/onboarding-requests`, `/support-threads`.

## Verification done
